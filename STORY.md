# Case File 4: Two Offices

Portside's trail led Priya Okonkwo-Reyes to a small regional branch —
nothing glamorous, just a front desk workstation and, somewhere behind
it, a back office nobody from outside the branch ever has reason to
touch. Her note says she "never found a direct line in" to the back
office — only a way through the front desk first.

She left two things behind at this branch: a case file, and a locked
file she calls `final.enc` — sitting in plain sight on the front desk's
own portal the entire time, openable only once you've been to both
rooms and brought something back from each.

Her note: "Don't assume the interesting host is the one you can see."
